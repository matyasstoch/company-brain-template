# Next Big Project: Non-Negotiables and Checklist

Answer every line here before committing to a new big project.

## Non-Negotiables

- Solve your own problem (be the #1 user)
- 10x better than the current solution
- Choose a specific target person
- Obsess over retention and real usage
- Make a small group of users love it before mass promotion
- Optimize for weekly growth
- Pick one concrete problem to solve
- Use new technology or a clear shift
- Use an unfair advantage you already have
- Build fast, kill fast

## Checklist

- Write down every key assumption in `assumptions.md`
- State the core thesis in 3 plain sentences
- Is this a real painkiller or just nice to have?
- Is this working with or against where the market is going?
- Have you personally tried all existing solutions?
- Are you using your real advantages?
- Do you have high conviction to stick with this for 12+ months?
