# Project Assumptions

List the assumptions that must be true for your next big project to work. Review them every month and mark each one proved or disproved.

## Example

1. Target users already spend at least $50/month trying to solve this problem. (Status: unverified)
2. Users can get their first useful output in under 5 minutes without a tutorial. (Status: unverified)
3. We can reach our first 20 users directly without paid ads. (Status: unverified)
