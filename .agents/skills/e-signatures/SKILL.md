# e-signatures

Pick one e-signature tool and stick with it. Our default is **Google eSignature** (in Google Drive) or **DocuSign**.

## Rules

- Default to Google eSignature. Use DocuSign only if the other party asks for it.
- Do not waste time comparing other tools. Speed beats a slightly nicer interface.
- Copy the other party's signature block exactly when they send one.
- Use your local calendar date when dating documents.
- Save the completed PDF and its audit trail right next to the original document in your legal repo.

## Google eSignature on a PDF

1. Open Google Drive and upload the PDF.
2. Open the PDF. Top right menu > **eSignature**.
3. Add each signer's name and email. Put your signer first if signing order matters.
4. Place a Signature field (and Name or Date field if needed) on each signer's line.
5. Click **Request signature**, add a short message, and send.
6. Save the signed PDF and audit trail back into Git once everyone signs.
